our story

Born from quiet mornings and the green outside the window

Lumié began with a simple belief: home should feel like an exhale. We take our cues from gardens, soft light and the small rituals that make an ordinary day feel kind.

Every piece is made in small batches with plant-based waxes, natural fragrance oils and honest materials. Nothing loud, nothing hurried, just things that are lovely to live with.
